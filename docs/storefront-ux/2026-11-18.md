## Research reviewed

- [Baymard — Usability Testing of Inline Form Validation (Jan 9, 2024)](https://baymard.com/research-articles/inline-form-validation) — validate after appropriate field completion, avoid premature errors, and remove resolved errors.
- [Baymard — Adaptive Validation Error Messages (Dec 14, 2023)](https://baymard.com/research-articles/adaptive-validation-error-messages) — make the correction specific to the actual problem.
- [Nielsen Norman Group — 10 Design Guidelines for Reporting Errors in Forms](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/errors-forms-design-guidelines/) — position actionable error text adjacent to the affected field.
- [Nielsen Norman Group — Placeholders in Form Fields Are Harmful](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/form-design-placeholders/) — visible labels should remain after entry.

These are research findings, not proof that the design is accessible or that any third-party checkout was fully audited. Amazon, Apple, Nike, Christianbook and Crossway were not independently audited this run.
