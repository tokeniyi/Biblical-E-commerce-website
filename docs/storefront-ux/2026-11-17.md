## Research reviewed

- [Baymard — Checkout flow optimization](https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-ux-optimization): actionable field errors belong adjacent to their inputs; preserve user input when errors occur; use an error summary for multiple problems.
- [Baymard — Inline form validation](https://baymard.com/research-articles/inline-form-validation): validate at an appropriate time and remove resolved errors rather than interrupt incomplete typing.
- [Baymard — Current checkout UX](https://baymard.com/research-articles/current-state-of-checkout-ux): make required and optional field status explicit.
- [Christianbook — NLT Pew Bible product](https://www.christianbook.com/nlt-pew-bible-blue-hardcover/9781414302027/pd/302020): live product detail shows format, text size, dimensions, ISBN, stock and binding; relevant to future product-detail refinement, not the checkout change made today.

No claim is made that Amazon, Apple, Nike, or Crossway were audited today.

## UX problem prioritized

In the existing first mobile checkout error state, the email-specific error was shown well below the address field instead of next to the invalid email. This makes the field-error association harder to scan and conflicts with the research-backed recommendation for immediate, local correction.
