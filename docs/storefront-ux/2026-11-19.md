## Research and references
- [Baymard, Inline Form Validation (Jan 9, 2024)](https://baymard.com/research-articles/inline-form-validation) — avoid premature errors; remove errors after correction.
- [Baymard, Adaptive Validation Error Messages (Dec 14, 2023)](https://baymard.com/research-articles/adaptive-validation-error-messages) — specify the exact correction instead of a generic failure.
- [Nielsen Norman Group, 10 Design Guidelines for Reporting Errors in Forms](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/errors-forms-design-guidelines/) — do not communicate errors by color alone; use an icon and keep messages adjacent to fields.
- [Baymard, Mobile Forms: Avoid Inline Labels](https://baymard.com/research-articles/mobile-forms-avoid-inline-labels) — retain labels outside filled inputs.

These are sourced principles, **not** evidence that Amazon, Apple, Nike, Christianbook or Crossway were audited today.

## UX problem prioritized
The email validation banner already used color and specific corrective text, but lacked a non-color error marker. This reduces scanability and may be less clear to users who cannot distinguish the error styling.
