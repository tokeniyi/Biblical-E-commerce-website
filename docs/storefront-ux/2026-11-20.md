## Research and references
- [Baymard — Usability Testing of Inline Form Validation (Jan 9, 2024)](https://baymard.com/research-articles/inline-form-validation) — avoid premature validation and remove resolved errors.
- [Baymard — Improve Validation Errors with Adaptive Messages (Dec 14, 2023)](https://baymard.com/research-articles/adaptive-validation-error-messages) — correction text should identify the specific fault.
- [Baymard — Mobile Forms: Avoid Inline Labels](https://baymard.com/research-articles/mobile-forms-avoid-inline-labels) — keep field labels visible after typing.
- [Nielsen Norman Group — Error-Message Guidelines](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/error-message-guidelines/) — explain errors in plain language with recovery guidance.
- [Nielsen Norman Group — Website Forms Usability](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/web-form-design/) — use multiple error cues and preserve entered input.

These are research observations, not a new direct audit of Amazon, Apple, Nike, Christianbook, or Crossway. No unsupported production-site behavior is asserted.
