## Research and references

- [Nike — Men's Shoes](https://www.nike.com/w/mens-shoes-4fe9cz9hh0lznik1zy7ok): the listing exposes grouped, category-specific filters for gender, sale/offers, color, price, size, brand, collections, sports, shoe height and closure type, alongside sorting. The useful observation is the explicit grouping of relevant facets, not the apparel values themselves.
- [Christianbook — Bibles](https://www.christianbook.com/page/bibles?event=FOOT): separates browse categories such as Audio, Compact & Pocket, Journaling, Large / Giant Print, Study and Text & Reference from a distinct “Refine by Translation” group. This supports keeping translation separate from physical format/purpose.
- [Christianbook — ESV Study Bible product page](https://www.christianbook.com/esv-study-bible-genuine-leather-black/9781433502446/pd/502440): the page exposes sample pages, product title, publisher/date/format, availability, price, reviews, quantity and Add to Cart; its product information also includes binding, text size, dimensions, page count and ISBN. These are edition-level details, not safe assumptions for an unverified catalog.
- [Crossway — ESV Compact Bible](https://www.crossway.org/bibles/esv-compact-bible-tru-18/): the page separates Bible categories and key features, shows availability and retail price, offers other cover styles, and lists format, type size, page layout, page count, dimensions, weight and ISBN.
- [Apple — Compare iPhone models](https://www.apple.com/iphone/compare/?modelList=iphone-14,iphone-15-pro,iphone-13): provides a model selector and aligned feature/price rows for comparing configurations. This is a comparison-structure reference only; no Apple layout was copied.
- [Baymard — Product Lists & Filtering UX](https://baymard.com/research/ecommerce-product-lists): its research treats list presentation, filtering and sorting as a connected product-finding system. [Filters for displayed list-item information](https://baymard.com/research-articles/have-filters-for-list-item-info) explains why attributes shown in results should also be filterable; [mobile product-list examples](https://baymard.com/mcommerce-usability/benchmark/mobile-page-types/product-list) documents the information-density tradeoff on small screens.
- [NN/G — Ecommerce Search, Filters and Routing Pages](https://www.nngroup.com/reports/ecommerce-ux-search-including-faceted-search/): covers search results, category/listing pages and faceted filters as the product-discovery path. [NN/G — Ecommerce Product Pages](https://www.nngroup.com/reports/ecommerce-ux-product-pages-including-reviews/) covers product specifications, comparison, options, imagery, price and availability.
- **Amazon limitation:** direct Amazon storefront access was blocked by robots.txt during research. No current Amazon page pattern is claimed or attributed in this report.

## UX problems prioritized

1. **Selected state relied on color alone.** The two selected checkboxes had blue fills but no check glyph, making the state less explicit.
2. **Touch targets were undersized.** The 23 × 23 px checkbox visuals and text-only Close/Reset controls did not meet the repository's 44 × 44 px minimum.
3. **Keyboard focus was specified but not drawn.** The documented initial focus on Close lacked a visible 2 px focus indicator in the open-drawer state.
